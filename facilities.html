Where Classes Come Alive

FITNESS STUDIOS

Two dedicated studios purpose-built for different training styles. Studio A features a sprung hardwood floor ideal for high-impact classes. Studio B is a serene, lower-impact space for yoga, pilates, and barre.

  • Sprung hardwood floor (impact-absorbing)
  • Full mirror walls
  • JBL premium sound system
  • Yoga mats, blocks & straps provided

Rest & Recovery

SAUNA & RECOVERY

Recovery is half the battle. Our wellness suite features a Finnish dry sauna, a eucalyptus steam room, and a cold plunge pool — the complete recovery trifecta used by elite athletes worldwide.

  • Finnish dry sauna (170–185°F)
  • Eucalyptus steam room
  • Cold plunge pool (55°F)
  • Stretching & foam rolling area
