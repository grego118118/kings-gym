OUR CLASS OFFERINGS

Boot Camp

High-intensity interval training with a military-inspired structure. Full-body conditioning using bodyweight and equipment.

45–60 min·All levels

Yoga Flow

Dynamic vinyasa yoga linking breath with movement. Builds flexibility, balance, and mental clarity. Modifications offered for all levels.

60–75 min·Beginner friendly

HIIT Express

Maximum effort in minimum time. 30-minute high-intensity intervals designed to torch calories and boost metabolism all day.

30 min·Intermediate

Spin / Indoor Cycling

Instructor-led cycling to high-energy music. Adjustable resistance for all fitness levels. Pure cardio endurance training.

45 min·All levels

Strength Circuit

Structured resistance training in a circuit format. Focus on compound lifts, muscular endurance, and progressive overload.

60 min·All levels

Kickboxing

Cardio kickboxing combining martial arts techniques with full-body fitness. High energy, empowering, and a serious calorie burn.

60 min·All levels

Pilates Core

Low-impact, precision-focused core work. Strengthens the deep stabilizers of the spine. Great for injury prevention and posture.

45 min·Beginner friendly

Barre Fusion

Ballet-inspired conditioning targeting the legs, glutes, and core. Small isometric movements that create big definition and strength.

55 min·Beginner friendly

Restorative Yoga

Gentle, prop-supported poses held for deep relaxation and recovery. Perfect for rest days and anyone needing stress relief.

75 min·All levels
