Good To Know

BEFORE YOU TAN

Wear Eye Protection

Protective goggles are required and available at the front desk. Never tan with your eyes closed alone — UV passes right through your eyelids.

Start Slow

If you're new or fair-skinned, begin with shorter sessions and build up gradually. Our staff will help you choose the right time for your skin type.

Hydrate & Moisturize

Drink water and moisturize before and after to keep your skin healthy and your tan looking its best for longer.
