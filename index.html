What We Offer

WORLD-CLASS FACILITIES

Weight Room

Multiple squat racks, Olympic barbells, dumbbells, and a dedicated lifting platform with Kings Gym floor branding. Built for serious lifters.

Cardio Floor

Rows of ellipticals, bikes, treadmills, and cable machines with TVs throughout. Whether you're warming up or going all out, we've got you covered.

Functional Training

Dedicated turf track with a Rogue sled for push/pull conditioning. Perfect for speed work, athletic training, and full-body circuits.

Boxing Zone

UFC heavy bag, cable station, and dedicated combat training space. Hit hard, train smart. Open to all members anytime.

Smoothie Bar

Fresh protein smoothies made in-house. Strawberry Bomb, Peach Mango, Peanut Butter Cup, and more. Add-ons: extra protein, creatine, PB. Buy 3 for 10% off.

Machine Floor

Dozens of Cybex selectorized machines covering every muscle group. Clean, well-maintained, and always available — even during peak hours.
