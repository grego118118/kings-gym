Have Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Is there a joining fee?
Monthly members pay a one-time $40 enrollment fee. Annual and 6-month members pay no enrollment fee — just the membership rate.
Can I try before I buy?
Yes! We offer a free one-day pass for first-time visitors. Stop by the front desk with a valid photo ID and we'll set you up at no charge.
How do I cancel my membership?
Monthly members may cancel at any time with 30 days' written notice. Annual and 6-month members may cancel after their term ends. We keep it simple — no runaround.
Are classes included in my membership?
Yes — all group fitness classes are included in every Kings Gym membership at no additional charge. This includes yoga, HIIT, boot camp, spin, and more.
Do you offer a first-month-free promotion?
Yes! New members who sign up for any plan currently receive their first month free. This offer is valid until June 30, 2026. No promo code needed — just mention it at sign-up.
